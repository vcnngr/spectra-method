# Step 07 - Handoff And Closure

## STEP GOAL

Create the final AppSec handoff for engineering, Blue Team, GRC, and reporting.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Redact secrets and production personal data.
- Route detection opportunities to Signal or `spectra-detection-lifecycle`.
- Route privacy exposure to Counsel or `spectra-privacy-breach-assessment`.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: do not include reusable exploit instructions in the report.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append:

- executive summary
- prioritized remediation plan
- detection opportunities
- risk acceptance candidates
- retest checklist
- final evidence index

SUCCESS: The workflow ends with remediation-ready AppSec evidence.

FAILURE: Report cannot support engineering action or management decision.
