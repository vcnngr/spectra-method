# Step 01 - Privacy Case Intake

## STEP GOAL

Create the privacy case record and separate known facts from assumptions.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Record source incident, affected systems, data classes, and decision owners.
- Mark qualified counsel status.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: do not create legal conclusions or public statements.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append case ID, incident reference, known facts, assumptions, data categories, stakeholders, and counsel gate.

SUCCESS: Privacy case has a factual baseline.

FAILURE: Assessment begins from unsourced or speculative facts.
