# Step 06 - Risk And Remediation

## STEP GOAL

Convert privacy findings into control gaps, remediation actions, and governance improvements.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Tie remediation to data minimization, access control, retention, logging, vendor, and process gaps.
- Assign owners and evidence.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: do not fabricate control attestations.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append gap, privacy risk, control owner, remediation, due date, evidence, and validation method.

SUCCESS: Privacy risk becomes owned work.

FAILURE: Recommendations are generic or unactionable.
