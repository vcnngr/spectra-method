# Step 03 - Jurisdiction And Clock Map

## STEP GOAL

Map potentially relevant regimes, notification clocks, discovery dates, and decision deadlines for counsel review.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Treat this as governance preparation, not legal advice.
- Mark uncertainty and require counsel confirmation.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: do not assert final legal applicability.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append jurisdiction, regime, possible clock trigger, known dates, evidence, owner, and counsel confirmation status.

SUCCESS: Decision owners can see time-sensitive obligations.

FAILURE: Clock starts or regimes are asserted without review.
