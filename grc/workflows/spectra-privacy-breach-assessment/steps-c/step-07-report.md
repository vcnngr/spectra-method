# Step 07 - Privacy Assessment Report

## STEP GOAL

Finalize the privacy and breach governance artifact.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Keep legal advice boundary visible.
- Minimize personal data.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: do not include final notice text unless counsel approved it.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append factual summary, exposure table, jurisdiction/clock map, legal hold status, decision log, remediation roadmap, and open questions.

SUCCESS: Artifact supports counsel, leadership, and remediation teams.

FAILURE: Report contains unsupported legal conclusions or excess personal data.
