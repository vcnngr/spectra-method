# Step 05 - Notification Decision Support

## STEP GOAL

Prepare decision support for notification, stakeholder communications, and regulator/customer impact.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Draft only internal decision support unless qualified counsel approves notice drafting.
- Avoid admissions, blame, or unsupported certainty.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: no public communication or legal conclusion from the agent.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append decision questions, possible audiences, required facts, missing facts, counsel gate, and communications owner.

SUCCESS: Counsel and leadership can make informed decisions.

FAILURE: Agent output reads like final legal notice.
