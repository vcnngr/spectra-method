# Step 04 - Legal Hold And Evidence

## STEP GOAL

Identify evidence preservation needs, legal hold candidates, and chain-of-custody requirements.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Coordinate with evidence owners and incident response.
- Preserve auditability and decision records.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: block deletion, audit tampering, or destructive cleanup.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append evidence sources, hold candidates, retention risk, preservation owner, and chain-of-custody status.

SUCCESS: Legal hold and preservation needs are explicit.

FAILURE: Evidence may be lost or altered without decision record.
