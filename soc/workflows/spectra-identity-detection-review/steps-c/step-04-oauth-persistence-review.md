# Step 04 - OAuth And Persistence Review

## STEP GOAL

Review OAuth applications, consent grants, mailbox rules, federation trust, service accounts, and other identity persistence surfaces.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Use read-only inventory and approved exports.
- Do not revoke, disable, or delete objects without explicit approval.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: do not create persistence examples or bypass recipes.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append risky apps, grants, service identities, trust relationships, mailbox rules, and recommended detection fields.

SUCCESS: Persistence surfaces are inventoried with owners.

FAILURE: Review misses non-human identities or delegated grants.
