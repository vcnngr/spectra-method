# Step 03 - Session And MFA Review

## STEP GOAL

Assess session controls, MFA posture, conditional access, token lifetime, impossible travel, and risky sign-in controls.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Review policy and telemetry evidence.
- Do not trigger live MFA prompts against real users.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: block account lockout or disruptive challenge testing.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append MFA gaps, bypass conditions, session risk, conditional access coverage, and telemetry needed for detection.

SUCCESS: Session and MFA risks are evidence-backed.

FAILURE: Findings depend on disruptive tests.
