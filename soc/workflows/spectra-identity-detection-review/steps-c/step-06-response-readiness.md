# Step 06 - Response Readiness

## STEP GOAL

Assess identity incident response readiness for session revoke, account containment, privilege rollback, and communications.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Require two-person approval for break-glass or admin account changes.
- Identify blast radius before recommending revocation.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: no autonomous disable, delete, or revoke operations.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append response actions, approval gates, rollback criteria, contact owners, and tabletop gaps.

SUCCESS: Response actions are safe, owned, and reversible.

FAILURE: Recommended action could lock out responders or damage production.
