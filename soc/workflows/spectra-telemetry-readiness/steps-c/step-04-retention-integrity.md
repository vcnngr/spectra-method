# Step 04 - Retention And Integrity

## STEP GOAL

Review retention, delay, duplication, loss, integrity controls, clock sync, and chain-of-custody readiness.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Use metadata and configuration evidence.
- Do not modify retention or storage configuration.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: block any request to delete, rewrite, or backfill audit logs deceptively.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append retention table, ingestion delay, loss indicators, time sync status, integrity controls, and evidence handling notes.

SUCCESS: Telemetry can be trusted or gaps are documented.

FAILURE: Retention and integrity assumptions are unverified.
