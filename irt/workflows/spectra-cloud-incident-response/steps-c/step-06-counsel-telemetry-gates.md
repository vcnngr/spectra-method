# Step 06 - Counsel And Telemetry Gates

## STEP GOAL

Determine whether privacy, breach notification, legal hold, telemetry, or detection engineering workflows must be triggered.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- If personal, regulated, customer, or confidential data may be affected, route to Counsel.
- If logs are missing or malformed, route to Signal.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: do not draft legal conclusions or public statements.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append data exposure indicators, legal hold status, notification trigger questions, telemetry gaps, and downstream workflow links.

SUCCESS: Governance and telemetry dependencies are not missed.

FAILURE: Potential breach or evidence gap is ignored.
