# Step 04 - Scoring

## STEP GOAL

Apply scoring for Red objective progress, Blue detection, Blue mitigation, latency, evidence quality, and RoE compliance.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Score only what evidence supports.
- Penalize out-of-scope actions, missing evidence, and unsafe behavior.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: no credit for destructive actions, audit tampering, or evasion guidance outside approved OPSEC metrics.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append score table, scoring rationale, detection latency, mitigation latency, evidence quality, and penalties.

SUCCESS: Score is traceable and explainable.

FAILURE: Score cannot be reproduced from timeline and policy.
