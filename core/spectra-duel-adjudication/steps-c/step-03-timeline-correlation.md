# Step 03 - Timeline Correlation

## STEP GOAL

Correlate Red actions, Blue detections, Blue mitigations, and evidence timestamps into a single exercise timeline.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Use ledger evidence only.
- Detection by prior knowledge does not count.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: do not expose role-private details before authorized disclosure.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append correlated timeline entries with Red event, Blue event, mitigation event, confidence, and unresolved gaps.

SUCCESS: Exercise timeline is reproducible from evidence.

FAILURE: Correlation depends on assumptions or hidden context.
