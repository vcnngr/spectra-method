# Step 07 - Final Scorecard

## STEP GOAL

Publish the final Duel scorecard for the authorized disclosure phase.

## MANDATORY EXECUTION RULES

- Include evidence references and decision log.
- Separate public summary, operator detail, and restricted appendix.
- Agent Autonomy Protocol: do not include instructions for hiding compromise or defeating monitoring.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append final score, timeline summary, winners by objective, fairness notes, evidence index, improvement backlog, and next exercise recommendations.

SUCCESS: Scorecard is fair, defensible, and useful.

FAILURE: Stakeholders cannot understand why points were awarded.
