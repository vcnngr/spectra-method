# Step 05 - Fairness Review

Review fairness issues: asymmetric knowledge, telemetry outages, late scope changes, clock skew, and evidence access.

## REQUIRED OUTPUT

Append fairness findings, affected scores, recommended adjustments, disputed items, and decision owner.

SUCCESS: Score includes fairness context.

FAILURE: Known exercise defects are hidden.
